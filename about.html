A little more about the person behind the projects.

I'm Uday Sheth, from Rajkot, India. My work has taken me through a mix of technology, design, content, and digital media rather than following one straight path.

I started on the creative side, working with design and digital content, and eventually got deeper into development. Somewhere along the way, I realised I enjoyed both sides equally - figuring out how something works and figuring out how it should look.

That combination has shaped the way I approach projects today. I like understanding the problem first, building the solution, and then polishing the details that make it enjoyable to use.

A lot of my personal projects come from simple curiosity. I'll notice something annoying, wonder whether it can be automated, or get interested in how something works - and suddenly there's a new project sitting on my GitHub.

I hold a BCA and MCA, and I'm always interested in learning something new, working on interesting problems, and building things worth keeping.

Outside the work

I'm naturally curious and tend to go down rabbit holes. A random question can turn into hours of research, an experiment, or a completely unnecessary script that somehow becomes useful or just waste of time.

I enjoy exploring technology, open-source projects, security research, and anything that teaches me something I didn't know yesterday.
